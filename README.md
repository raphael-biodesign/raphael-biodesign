### Hi, I'm Raphael

I run [Biodesign Academy](https://biodesign.academy), from Stockholm and London. We help designers who work with living matter become [AI-native biodesigners](https://www.biodesign.academy/ai-native-biodesigner): designers who work with AI throughout a project, not just at one step, while staying close to the organisms and matter they make with.

We also share open work here as it develops, starting with [Protein Backstory](https://raphael-biodesign.github.io/protein-backstory/), on where biological data comes from and who it is owed to.
